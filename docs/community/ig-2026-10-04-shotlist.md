# IG carousel — shot list (version picker, Shakedown Street)

Capture these yourself on the phone: the sandbox can reach neither the live
site nor Supabase, so anything rendered here would be fallback fonts over an
empty page. These are the real screens.

## Before you shoot — two things that will bite

1. **Wait for the publish to finish**, then hard-refresh. Shooting the old
   build photographs the bug this release fixes.
2. **Save a FRESH listening guide.** Guides saved before tonight still carry
   bare prose with no night attached, so they stay on the old by-title lookup
   and can show the wrong tape or none. A guide you create *after* the deploy
   binds each night properly. Do not photograph an old one.

Shoot at 4:5 (1080×1350) or crop to it. Instagram centre-crops a tall
screenshot and will slice the stats line off a 19.5:9 phone grab.
Scroll once before capturing so Safari's URL bar collapses.

## Slide 1 — the song, and the scale of it
`/versions/shakedown-street`, scrolled to the head.
Must be visible: the song title, how many times they played it, the span of
years. This is the "oh, that many?" beat.

Alternative worth trying first: tap the **Instagram share door** on this page.
It renders a purpose-built 1080×1350 card (`renderVersionsCard`) with real
numbers and the correct brand faces — it was built for exactly this slot and
will beat a screenshot. Use it as slide 1 and move the screenshot to slide 2.

## Slide 2 — first and last
The two chips: **"First time played"** and **"Last time played"**, each with
its date and venue.
If either night has no tape, the persistent line on screen reads **"No tape of
this night circulates — the date is on record, the music isn't."** Keep it in
frame if it appears: it is the honest part and reads as credibility.
(The shorter "…circulates yet" wordings are toasts that flash on tap. Don't
plan a shot around them — they'll be gone before you capture.)

## Slide 3 — the nights to choose from
The row of nights, dates and venues visible.

**Shakedown will NOT show a quiet gem.** That mark is only for songs with no
fan-vote data, and Shakedown has fifteen ranked nights from headyversion, so
its cards carry **"Era benchmark"** and **"Sleeper"** instead. A sleeper polls
under 30% of the vote leader; a gem is a tape held high and pulled least.
Different claims — don't caption a sleeper as a gem. Frame whichever chip you
actually see.

Likewise **"Start with the one they all name"** renders only when the lead
night isn't the debut. For Shakedown you'll most likely see **"The very first
time they played it"**. Shoot what's on screen.

## Slide 4 — the kept guide
The saved listening guide: the nights in reading order, debut opening it,
farewell closing it. Shoot it after it saves, from the guide view.
