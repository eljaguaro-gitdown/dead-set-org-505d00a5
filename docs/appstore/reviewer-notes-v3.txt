Thank you for reviewing Dead Set.

ACCOUNT DELETION (guideline 5.1.1(v))
Menu (top right) > Delete Account > "Delete My Account" > confirm. The
account and its data are permanently deleted in the app; no website, email or
support contact is involved.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT (guideline 1.2)
Users publish setlists, comments and direct messages.
- Terms: signing in or registering requires ticking agreement to the Terms of
  Use, which state zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users.
- Filter: titles, comments, messages and display names pass an
  objectionable-language filter, in the app and again on our server.
- Report: the flag beside a setlist's curator, on every comment, in every
  message thread and on every profile.
- Block: the same flag, or the icon beside a comment. Blocking removes that
  user's setlists and comments from the blocker's feeds at once, stops their
  messages, and notifies us.
- Every report emails our moderation team. We act within 24 hours: the content
  is removed and the account that posted it is banned.

WHAT THE APP IS AND WHO IT IS FOR
A setlist-building and listening companion for Grateful Dead fans. The band
let audiences record and freely trade its roughly 2,300 concerts, so tens of
thousands of recordings sit in the public Internet Archive, organised by date.
Dead Set lets a fan assemble a "dream setlist" -- a specific night's
performance of each song, arranged as a concert with Set I, Set II and an
Encore -- then play it back and share it. The audience is adult music fans.
The app is free: no ads, no purchases, no monetisation.

SETUP AND ACCESS
No setup required. Browsing and listening need no account; an account unlocks
building, favourites and comments. The demo credentials are an email/password
account needing no Google or Apple ID. Home shows community setlists: tap one,
press play. Browse or "Build with Cosmic Charlie" starts a setlist.

AUDIO CONTENT AND RIGHTS (guideline 5.2)
Dead Set is an independent, non-commercial fan project, not affiliated with,
endorsed by or sponsored by the Grateful Dead, Grateful Dead Productions,
Rhino Entertainment or Warner Music Group. All trademarks belong to their
owners. Stated in the app footer and at https://dead-set.org/about.

The Grateful Dead permitted audience taping and free non-commercial trading
of their live performances, which is why the Internet Archive's Live Music
Archive hosts these recordings publicly. We host no audio, stream from the
Archive's public endpoints, carry no advertising and sell nothing. At the
band's request the Archive makes soundboards stream-only; Dead Set offers no
download anywhere and never requests a restricted original. The band's policy
is reproduced in-app at https://dead-set.org/about. Same model as Relisten
(App Store ID 715886886), on the store since 2014.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
Internet Archive (audio). Relisten (api.relisten.net, song order). Supabase
via Lovable Cloud (accounts, database, storage). Lovable AI Gateway, the
language model behind the in-app setlist assistant, which receives a user's
typed request and setlist contents. PostHog, first-party product analytics
with no advertising or cross-app tracking, so App Tracking Transparency does
not apply. Resend for transactional and opt-in email. No payment processors.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. Identical in every region.

ACCOUNT (guideline 4.8)
Sign in with Apple, Google, or email and password. Sign in with Apple sits
alongside Google on the same screen.

BACKGROUND AUDIO
The app declares the audio background mode and plays with lock-screen
transport via the system player: the core use case is listening to full
concert sets while the phone is pocketed or locked.
