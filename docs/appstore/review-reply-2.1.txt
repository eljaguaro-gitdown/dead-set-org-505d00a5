2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Dead Set is for Grateful Dead fans. The band played roughly 2,300 concerts
between 1965 and 1995 and let audiences record and freely trade them, so
tens of thousands of concert recordings sit in the public Internet Archive.
It is organised by date, which makes finding a particular performance of a
particular song hard and comparing performances across years nearly
impossible. Dead Set lets a fan assemble a "dream setlist" - a specific
night's performance of each song, arranged as a real concert with Set I, Set
II and an Encore - then play it back and share it. The audience is adult
music fans. The app is free, with no ads, no purchases and no monetisation.

3. SETUP AND ACCESS
No setup required. Browsing and listening need no account; an account
unlocks building, favourites and comments. The demo credentials are an
email/password account needing no Google or Apple ID; Sign in with Apple and
Google are offered on the same screen. Home shows setlists: tap one, press play. Browse or "Build with Cosmic
Charlie" starts a setlist; the version browser picks the exact night per
song. Reporting is the flag on any setlist, comment or direct message;
blocking is the icon beside another person's comment; deletion is Profile >
Danger Zone.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Internet Archive (archive.org): source of all audio, streamed from their
  public endpoints. See item 6.
- Relisten (api.relisten.net): public concert database, used for song order.
- Supabase, via Lovable Cloud: accounts, database, storage, server functions.
- Lovable AI Gateway: the language model behind our in-app setlist
  assistant, which suggests songs when a user asks for help building a set.
  It receives the user's typed request and setlist contents.
- PostHog: first-party product analytics. No advertising and no cross-app
  tracking, so App Tracking Transparency does not apply.
- Resend: transactional and opt-in email.
No payment processors, as nothing is sold.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. Identical in every region: no geographic gating, no region-specific
content, free everywhere.

Dead Set is an independent, non-commercial fan project, not affiliated with,

The Grateful Dead explicitly permitted audience taping and free
non-commercial trading of their live performances throughout their career,
which is why the Internet Archive's Live Music Archive hosts these
recordings publicly. We honour the non-commercial terms: we host no audio,

anywhere, and on items marked access-restricted or stream-only it plays the
Archive's own streaming derivative, never the restricted original. The
band's policy is reproduced verbatim in-app at the same URL.
