- **In-app announcement:** `useAnnouncements.ts`/`AnnouncementsBell.tsx` do track per-user reads (`announcement_reads` table) and CTA clicks (`trackCtaClick` into `share_events`) - this is real, structured data, just currently unreachable (RLS plus no service-role key this session).

I traced what that edge function persists (`ai-deadhead/index.ts` around line 566): `cosmic_charlie_history` stores only `song_title` and `generated_at` (plus user/visitor id) - the priority/preference input itself is never persisted anywhere, structured or not. Even with full Supabase and PostHog access restored, "how many people picked Firsts and Lasts" cannot currently be answered. This is the top gap flagged in the task brief, confirmed.

- **`/backstage` visits: the one signal that's actually fully instrumented today.** `VisitorTracker.tsx` fires `track-visit` on every route app-wide, and PostHog's pageview autocapture independently sees `/backstage` too. This just needs credentials to read - no code gap here.
