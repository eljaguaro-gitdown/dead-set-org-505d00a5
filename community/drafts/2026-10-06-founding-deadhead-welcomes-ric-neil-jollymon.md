## 2) Chip (welcome back)

**Subject:** Welcome back, Chip

Hey Now Chip,

**Set I**

Good to have you back in the room. Everything you hear on Dead Set still stands on the Internet Archive's Live Music Archive, which exists because tapers set up their reels and traders kept the tapes moving for decades. Every time you press play, you're hearing their work. We're one more way through that door.

**Set II**

A lot has happened since you were last around. The highlights:

- **The Songbook** (dead-set.org/songbook). Each week, one song followed across every era it lived through, from first time played to last, with the versions in between worth your evening.
- **Version ladders on every song.** Narrow a song's whole history to a stretch of years. "Play the sleepers" now actually queues the under-heard versions, oldest first.
- **Cosmic Charlie has sharper opinions.** He now judges a version against its own era, so a good '77 isn't measured by '72 standards. There's also a new "Firsts & Lasts" chip, and he'll go hunting for first-time-played and last-time-played songs.
- **New names for the "what kind of night?" chips:** Late Night, Daytime Show and Day Into Night.
- **Your set starts playing the moment it's built.** No pre-roll, straight into the music.
- **Dead Set is coming to the App Store.** We've submitted the iPhone app and are working through Apple's review now. Our beta circle already runs it through TestFlight: sign in with Google or Apple, and listen from your lock screen with the venue and date on the artist line.
- **Friends in Messages.** Tap a friend's name and you land on their public setlists.

**Encore**

I can't wait to hear what you think. Reply and tell me what you like, what feels off, and what you want next. Backstage (dead-set.org/backstage) is where those notes go straight into the build.

Gratefully in it with you,
